Natural voices in Huginn and Muninn

Read aloud speaks with one of two Piper voices (British English, medium quality), run on the device by the speech
engine below. Nothing is sent anywhere to speak: the voice is downloaded once, from this project's "voices-1" release,
checked against code/shared/voices.sha256, and kept.

Female: Alba (en_GB-alba-medium)
  Piper voice by Michael Hansen (rhasspy/piper-voices), trained on a dataset from the University of Edinburgh. License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0),
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Male: Northern English Male (en_GB-northern_english_male-medium)
  Piper voice by Michael Hansen (rhasspy/piper-voices). License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0
  International (CC BY-SA 4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/. The voice files are passed on
  unchanged, under the same license.

Both medium voices were fine-tuned from Piper's US English "lessac" voice, whose source recordings (Blizzard
Challenge 2013) carry a research license. Huginn and Muninn are free and the voices are never sold.
Each voice's MODEL_CARD travels inside its download.
