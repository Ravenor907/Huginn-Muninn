Ultra privacy: Tor, built into Huginn and Muninn

Huginn (Windows): tor.exe from the Tor Project's Tor Expert Bundle, signed by the Tor Browser developers.
Huginn (Flatpak): tor and libevent, built from their source in the Flatpak manifest.
Muninn (Android): the Guardian Project's tor-android (Tor with OpenSSL, libevent, zlib and zstd), from Maven.
