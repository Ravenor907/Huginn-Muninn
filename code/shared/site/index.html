Why anti-deplatforming

No account to close. No server to ban you.

  • No account, phone number or email. Your identity is a key made on your device, and only you hold it.
  • No central server. No company server in the middle stores your contacts or decides who may talk.
  • Many independent relays. Each message is sealed and published to several public Nostr relays at once (five by default). One going down doesn't matter.
  • Direct when close. Same Wi-Fi or Bluetooth: device to device, no internet needed.
  • Cloudflare, plainly. A free relay Worker hands out logins for Cloudflare's call relay, used for internet calls when a direct path isn't possible. Messages never go through it, and internet calls can be turned off.

Relays pass along sealed envelopes they can't read. Nothing in the middle can ban you.

Secure communication

Sealed for one person at a time

Everything is built on keys that live only on your devices.

Per message, per recipient

A ratchet per contact gives every message a fresh key. In groups each message is sealed separately to each member: no group key.

Forward secrecy with day keys

Relay envelopes are also sealed to a short-lived day key the recipient deletes, so a key stolen later can't open old traffic.

Signed group control

Members, roles and rules are a snapshot signed by an admin. Unsigned changes are refused.

Password-encrypted backups

scrypt and AES-256-GCM. There has never been an unencrypted backup.

App lock

A pattern or a password locks everything the app keeps, with a panic lock to close it at once.

Nothing held outside

Keys and chats live in the app's own encrypted vault, never in an OS key store or an outside service.

IP addresses never shown

Neither app ever puts an IP address on screen.

Safety codes, in person

A 6-digit code and 4 emoji per contact. Same on both screens means nobody is in between.

FAQ

Questions

No server? How does a message get there?
Over the internet, your app seals the message for the person it's for and publishes it to several public Nostr relays at once. Their app takes whichever copy arrives first. The relays can't read it. On the same Wi-Fi or over Bluetooth it goes straight from one device to the other.
What if a relay is shut down?
Nothing changes for you. Every message goes to all the relays at once, so the others still carry it, and your app keeps sending it again for a day until it is delivered.
Is it free?
Yes. Both apps are free and open source under GPL-3.0, with no ads and nothing to pay for.
How do I add someone?
Tap +, then Add someone. Face to face, scan their QR code with Muninn; from afar, paste the code or link they sent you. Then check your safety code together in person.
What does Cloudflare see?
Only for internet calls without a direct path: the call relay sees your public address and carries encrypted media it can't decode. The relay Worker that hands out its logins sees your address, a hash of a random id and the time. Turn off Internet calls and calls use only Wi-Fi and Bluetooth.
